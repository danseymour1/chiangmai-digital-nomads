**This is an example community story.** It exists only to show how stories look on the Community page and how the tag filter works. Delete `src/content/community/example-story.md` once real stories start coming in.

## How a real story would read

A real story would be written in the reader's own words, lightly tidied up. It might cover how they found somewhere to live, the café where they ended up doing most of their work, a meal they still think about, or what surprised them in their first few weeks.

## What to include when you publish one

- The reader's name or chosen credit in `author`.
- One or more tags from the list in `src/site.config.ts`, so the story shows up under the right filter.
- A cover photo from the reader, if they sent one, in `public/images/` with `image` and `imageAlt` set.

Stories like this appear only on the Community page, never in the guides list.
