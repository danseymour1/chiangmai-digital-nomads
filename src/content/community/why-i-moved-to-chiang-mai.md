By [Dan](https://danroundtheworld.com/why-i-moved-to-chiang-mai-as-a-digital-nomad/)

I had been travelling and working remotely for about 4 years, moving country roughly every month or whenever the tourist visa ended. I wasn't at the point, but I could tell I was going to get burnt out.

I decided I needed to semi settle somewhere, and with the new DTV that had just been released, I thought it would make sense to try somewhere in Thailand.

As I work in SEO, Chiang Mai seemed a good fit. I booked an Airbnb for 6 weeks to see if I would like it.

Spoiler: I loved it!

I met a great group of people straight away, I met people that worked in SEO, I joined a gym. With meeting so many new people and so many events going on, it was the most social I had been since my days at university!

![Dan with a group of friends holding water guns on a Chiang Mai street](/images/community/why-i-moved-to-chiang-mai/why-i-moved-to-chiang-mai-2.jpg)

Before those 6 weeks were up, I signed a contract on an apartment for a year as I knew it was the place I wanted to be.

After being in Chiang Mai for over a year, I've realised that the smoky season wasn't great but wasn't as bad as I was expecting. The rainy season wasn't as bad either. I've learned so much and grown as a person.

![A temple hall at Wat Chedi Luang in Chiang Mai under a cloudy sky, with colourful lanterns hanging between the columns](/images/community/why-i-moved-to-chiang-mai/why-i-moved-to-chiang-mai-3.jpg)

If anyone is thinking about coming to Chiang Mai to work as a digital nomad, the answer is definitely a yes!

## About Dan

Hey! I'm Dan from England. I've been in Chiang Mai since September 2025. I moved here because I work in SEO and run a travel blog, and it makes a great place to work on both of those at the same time.
