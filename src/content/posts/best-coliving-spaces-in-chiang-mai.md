Coliving in Chiang Mai means a private room, a coworking desk downstairs and a ready-made group of other remote workers, all on one bill. It costs more than renting a condo, but it removes the hardest parts of arriving in a new city. The city has only a handful of genuine coliving spaces, and the six below are the ones built around remote workers rather than ordinary hostels.

Prices are for a private room on a monthly stay unless stated, and most include the coworking space, wifi and cleaning. Compared with the rents in the [best areas to live in Chiang Mai](/best-areas-to-live-in-chiang-mai/), coliving carries a premium, so it suits a first stay best.

## 1. Alt_ChiangMai — best all-rounder

Alt_ChiangMai sits on Intrawarorot Road, behind Wat Phra Singh on the western side of the Old City. It combines coliving rooms, a 24-hour coworking space and a café, and is one of the most consistently recommended places to work in the city.

- **Price:** nightly rates start at around ฿1,445 and halve on stays of 29 nights or more, which puts a basic room at roughly ฿21,000–24,000 a month; six-night minimum stay.
- **Area:** Old City.
- **Coworking:** on site and open 24 hours, with a quiet upstairs floor; non-residents pay around ฿320 a day or ฿4,000 a month.
- **Who it suits:** remote workers who want a proper desk and a social community in equal measure.
- **Upsides:** strong work set-up, good coffee on site, regular events and meet-ups, walkable to temples and the [Sunday Walking Street](/what-to-see-and-do-in-chiang-mai/).
- **Downsides:** among the pricier options, and the Old City gets crowded with tourists in high season.

## 2. Alt_PingRiver — best for a riverside base

Alt_'s second site is at Wat Ket, on the east bank of the Ping River, a neighbourhood the city has recently promoted as a digital nomad zone. It follows the same model as the Old City branch, with a more spacious, newer building.

- **Price:** nightly rates from around ฿1,637, with the same long-stay discounts as Alt_ChiangMai, so roughly ฿24,000–26,000 a month; six-night minimum stay.
- **Area:** Wat Ket, riverside.
- **Coworking:** on site, with call booths, meeting rooms, private offices and a podcast room; non-resident passes from ฿220 a day or ฿4,000 a month.
- **Who it suits:** longer-stayers and couples who prefer a quieter, less touristy base.
- **Upsides:** calmer streets, river views nearby, yoga and member-led workshops, and a sauna listed among the wellness facilities.
- **Downsides:** further from Nimman's cafés, and the Night Bazaar area across the river is busy in the evenings.

## 3. Hub53 — best value

Hub53 is on Chonprathan Road in Suthep, about an eight-minute walk from Nimmanhaemin Road and MAYA. It is the most affordable private-room coliving space in this list, with eight room types published openly on its website.

- **Price:** ฿12,500–23,000 a month, or ฿2,900–6,200 a week; the cheapest rooms share a bathroom. Rates include a 24-hour coworking membership, electricity, water, weekly cleaning and unlimited coffee.
- **Area:** Suthep, beside Nimman.
- **Coworking:** on site, with open desks, private offices and a meeting room; non-members pay around ฿195 a day, or ฿1,999–3,399 a month depending on hours.
- **Who it suits:** budget-conscious nomads who want a social base close to Nimman.
- **Upsides:** utilities included, a well-equipped shared kitchen, a pleasant common area, an active WhatsApp community, and good street food within a two-minute walk.
- **Downsides:** the coworking tables are small and closely packed, and the cheapest rooms are only 9 square metres.

Monthly rates are offered only from 1 March to 30 September. During the high season, from October to February, Hub53 rents by the day or week only, which is worth factoring in alongside the [best time to visit Chiang Mai](/best-time-to-visit-chiang-mai/).

## 4. The Social Club — best for a busy social life

The Social Club occupies a building on a lane off Arak Road, inside the Old City walls on the side nearest Nimman. As the name suggests, the community calendar is the main draw.

- **Price:** a standard room is listed from around ฿28,500 a month, including coworking; rates are quoted through its booking page.
- **Area:** Old City.
- **Coworking:** on site, indoor and outdoor, with two silent rooms, phone rooms and monitors to rent.
- **Who it suits:** solo nomads who want to meet people quickly.
- **Upsides:** 21-square-metre rooms with a balcony, private bathroom and in-room desk; family dinners, game nights, hikes, yoga and trips out of town.
- **Downsides:** the most expensive option here, and the nightlife focus will not suit everyone.

There is an ice bath on site, and a gym and a Muay Thai gym within about five minutes' walk.

## 5. Socialer — best on a tight budget

Socialer is a small coliving and coworking space on Taeparak Road in Santitham, around 15 minutes' walk from Nimman. It sits somewhere between a hostel and a coliving space.

- **Price:** the cheapest in this list, with dorm beds and simple private rooms booked by the night; ask directly for long-stay rates.
- **Area:** Santitham.
- **Coworking:** a small ground-floor workspace, free for coliving residents; non-residents pay around ฿200 a day or ฿3,999 a month.
- **Who it suits:** backpackers and nomads doing a few hours' work a day.
- **Upsides:** low cost, fast wifi, residents get priority for events, and it is surrounded by cheap local food.
- **Downsides:** the workspace is small with mismatched furniture, so it is not a serious base for full-time workers.

Those who need a full working day may prefer to pair a bed here with one of the city's [laptop-friendly cafés](/best-cafes-work-chiang-mai/) or a separate [coworking membership](/best-coworking-spaces-in-chiang-mai/).

## 6. 4Seas Nimman — best for tech builders

4Seas is a coliving and coworking hub in Nimman with roots in the Ethereum and Zuzalu communities, and a second, quieter site called 4Seas Mountain View for slower stays.

- **Price:** on request.
- **Area:** Nimman.
- **Coworking:** on site, with quiet zones, phone booths, meeting rooms, a library and a content studio.
- **Who it suits:** founders, developers and anyone working in crypto or AI.
- **Upsides:** a weekly programme of talks and screenings, and a strong network for builders.
- **Downsides:** a niche focus, and no published prices.

## Is coliving cheaper than renting a condo?

No. A one-bedroom condo plus a monthly coworking pass usually costs less than a coliving room, as the [cost of living in Chiang Mai](/cost-of-living-chiang-mai-digital-nomads/) breakdown shows. What coliving buys is convenience: no deposit, no utility bills, no furniture and an instant social circle. Many nomads book a month in coliving, find their feet, then move into a condo.

## How to choose

- **Best all-round work and community:** Alt_ChiangMai.
- **Quieter riverside base:** Alt_PingRiver.
- **Lowest monthly price with a private room:** Hub53.
- **Busiest social calendar:** The Social Club.
- **Tightest budget:** Socialer.
- **Tech and crypto network:** 4Seas.

Book a week first where possible, then extend once the room, the desk and the people feel right. Most remote workers stay on a tourist or [Destination Thailand Visa](/digital-nomad-visas-chiang-mai/), but rules change, so check official Thai immigration and embassy sources before travelling.
